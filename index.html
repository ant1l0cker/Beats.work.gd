06 / FAQ

QUESTIONS

How do I receive my beat?

After you place an order and payment is confirmed on WhatsApp, your files are sent to you. Order status updates appear under My Orders.

What is the difference between Lease and Exclusive?

A lease gives you a WAV file and commercial streaming rights while the beat stays available to others. Exclusive gives you full ownership with stems, and the beat is taken off the store.

Can I preview a beat before buying?

Yes. Press play on any beat to hear the full preview. Previews are for listening only.

How do I pay?

Payment is arranged with me on WhatsApp right after you place your order.

Can I get a custom beat or bulk deal?

Yes. Message me on WhatsApp or Instagram with what you have in mind.
